\begin{abstract}
For a graph $G$, let $\bip(G)=|E(G)|-\mc(G)$ be the minimum number of
edges whose deletion makes $G$ bipartite.  We give an exact, independently
replayable order-$10$ flag-algebra certificate which, combined with the
per-root-MaxCut envelope and blow-up transfer of Ferudun and the density-tail
theorem of Balogh, Clemen and Lidick\'y, proves
\[
  \bip(G)\le |V(G)|^2/25
\]
for every finite triangle-free graph $G$.  The new exact dual has objective
$\delta_\star<-9/10000<0$.  It consists of
$10{,}188$ finitely described K7, K8 and rooted-Horn rows, together with the
public manifestly positive-semidefinite Gram functional.  A verifier rebuilds
all $11{,}560{,}170$ nonzero matrix entries and checks every dual residual in
exact rational arithmetic.  This manuscript and certificate are a draft for
external review.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

For a finite simple graph $G$, write
\[
  \bip(G)=\min\{|F|:F\subseteq E(G),\ G-F\text{ is bipartite}\}
          =|E(G)|-\mc(G).
\]
Erd\H{o}s, Gy\H{o}ri and Simonovits asked whether every triangle-free graph
$G$ on $N$ vertices satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:main}
  \bip(G)\le \frac{N^2}{25}.
\end{equation}
The constant is sharp: for $N=5n$, the balanced blow-up $C_5[n]$ has
$\bip(C_5[n])=n^2=N^2/25$.

Balogh, Clemen and Lidick\'y proved the exact constant in low- and
high-edge-density tails, and a slightly weaker general bound
\cite{BCL}.  Ferudun developed an order-$10$ per-root-MaxCut/Horn/Gram
certificate for the remaining middle-density band and, using integrality,
proved the conjectured equality for balanced $C_5$ multiples through
$N=200$ \cite{Ferudun}.  A later exact certificate extended this finite range
through $N=240$.  The present note completes the same certificate route: an
enlarged but still finite row pool has a strictly negative exact dual
objective.  Consequently no integrality margin is needed.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:main}
Every finite triangle-free graph $G$ on $N$ vertices satisfies
\[
  \bip(G)\le \frac{N^2}{25}.
\]
If $5\mid N$, equality is attained by the balanced blow-up of $C_5$.
\end{theorem}

\section{Blow-up transfer and the density split}

For an integer $t\ge1$, let $G[t]$ be the graph obtained by replacing each
vertex of $G$ by an independent set of size $t$ and every edge by the
corresponding complete bipartite graph.

\begin{lemma}[Ferudun]\label{lem:blowup}
For every finite graph $G$ and every $t\ge1$,
\[
  \bip(G[t])=t^2\bip(G).
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The cut value of $G[t]$, divided by $t^2$, is a multilinear function of the
fractions $x_v\in[0,1]$ of each twin class placed on one side.  A multilinear
function on a cube attains its maximum at a vertex.  Thus an optimal cut is
constant on every twin class, so $\mc(G[t])=t^2\mc(G)$.  Since
$|E(G[t])|=t^2|E(G)|$, the assertion follows.
\end{proof}

Define
\[
  \dmono(G)=\frac{2\bip(G)}{N^2},\qquad
  \dedge(G)=\frac{2|E(G)|}{N^2}.
\]
Lemma~\ref{lem:blowup} makes $\dmono$ blow-up invariant.  If $W_G$ is the
step graphon of $G$, then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:exact-transfer}
  \dmono(W_G)=\frac{2\bip(G)}{N^2}
\end{equation}
exactly; there is no finite-size error.

We use the closed middle band
\[
  \mathrm{LO}=\frac{1243}{5000}=0.2486,
  \qquad
  \mathrm{HI}=\frac{3197}{10000}=0.3197.
\]
Balogh, Clemen and Lidick\'y prove, for all sufficiently large orders, the
bound~\eqref{eq:main} when the edge density lies below LO or above HI
\cite[Theorem 1.3]{BCL}.  Applying their theorem to $G[t]$ and then using
Lemma~\ref{lem:blowup} transfers both exact-constant tail bounds to every
finite $G$ whose graphon-normalized density is strictly outside the closed
middle band.  The two boundary values will be included in the certificate
band.

\section{The finite relaxation}

We use the order-$10$ relaxation of \cite{Ferudun}.  Its probability vector
$q=(q_s)$ is indexed by the $12{,}172$ triangle-free order-$10$ states and
satisfies $q_s\ge0$, $\sum_s q_s=1$, and
$\mathrm{LO}\le\langle e,q\rangle\le\mathrm{HI}$.  A fixed manifestly
positive-semidefinite moment functional gives $\langle m,q\rangle\ge0$.

For each of the $107$ triangle-free K7 root types and each Boolean profile
rule $c$, the rule defines a genuine global two-coloring and hence a valid
per-root MaxCut row.  The analogous K8 construction has $410$ root types.
Writing $u^7_\sigma,u^8_\tau$ for the root envelope variables, the finite LP
contains selected valid rows
\[
 u^7_\sigma\le \langle g_{\sigma,c},q\rangle,
 \qquad
 u^8_\tau\le \langle h_{\tau,c},q\rangle,
\]
together with
\[
 \eta\le\sum_\sigma u^7_\sigma,
 \qquad
 \eta\le-\frac{2}{25}+\sum_\tau u^8_\tau.
\]
It also contains rooted-Horn inequalities.  Their validity is the
copositivity inequality
\[
  \left(\sum_{i=0}^4x_i\right)^2
      -4\sum_{i=0}^4x_ix_{i+1}\ge0
      \quad (x_i\ge0),
\]
with indices modulo $5$; equivalently it is the Motzkin-Straus inequality for
$C_5$.  Every genuine band step graphon induces a feasible point.  Therefore
the primal optimum upper-bounds
$\dmono(W)-2/25$ on the band.  A feasible dual of objective $\delta$ proves
\begin{equation}\label{eq:band-bound}
  \dmono(W)\le\frac{2}{25}+\delta.
\end{equation}
Only dual feasibility, not numerical optimality, is needed.

\section{Independent exact replay}

The primary certificate is a transparent JSON file.  The independent verifier
does not import the LP generator and does not trust a stored floating matrix.
It performs the following checks.

\begin{enumerate}
\item It validates all descriptor syntax and reconstructs every K7, K8 and
Horn functional from exact finite combinatorial data.
\item It builds an integer matrix of shape $10{,}188\times12{,}172$ with
$11{,}560{,}170$ nonzero entries.
\item It checks nonnegativity, the exact envelope-leg sum, and all 517
per-root multiplier lower bounds.
\item It computes the sparse transpose product by base-$100{,}000$ digit
splitting, with an a priori signed-64-bit bound, and audits nine columns by
direct Python-integer dot products.
\item It reconstructs all coordinate loads and the objective as exact
rational numbers and verifies that the minimum residual is zero and that
$\delta_\star<0$.
\end{enumerate}

Separately, the fixed moment vector is reconstructed from 87 nonnegative
rational rank-one Gram atoms and matches all $12{,}172$ coordinates exactly.
Both the JSON and pickle certificate representations pass the replay.  The
terminal output is
\begin{verbatim}

digit_product_audit=9/9
min_q_residual=0 at state=46
objective ~= -9.878886951679021e-04
global_closure=True
GLOBAL_CONCLUSION: beta(G)<=N^2/25 for every finite triangle-free graph
\end{verbatim}

\section{Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:main}}

Let $G$ be a triangle-free graph on $N$ vertices.

If $\dedge(G)<\mathrm{LO}$ or $\dedge(G)>\mathrm{HI}$, the transferred
Balogh-Clemen-Lidick\'y tail theorem gives $\bip(G)\le N^2/25$.

Otherwise $\mathrm{LO}\le\dedge(G)\le\mathrm{HI}$.  Applying the exact band
certificate to $W_G$ and using~\eqref{eq:exact-transfer} and
\eqref{eq:negative},
\[
  \frac{2\bip(G)}{N^2}=\dmono(W_G)
   \le\frac{2}{25}+\delta_\star
   <\frac{2}{25}.
\]
Hence $\bip(G)<N^2/25$, which is stronger than the required inequality in the
middle band.  These cases cover every possible density, including the two
band boundaries.  Finally, $C_5[n]$ shows equality for $N=5n$.
\qed

\section{Reproducibility and status}

The ancillary package contains the exact JSON and pickle certificates, the
independent replay, the moment-vector verifier, the floating source dual, the
descriptor pool, and scripts that regenerate the large K7 exact-count cache.
The compact certificate archive omits that cache.  A separate full replay
bundle includes the cache and all four public flag-data files needed by the
verifiers; it is about 20 MB compressed and about 1.8 GB unpacked.  The public
repository runs the full replay in continuous integration.

The finite certificate and fixed Gram vector have been independently replayed
inside the present computational environment in exact arithmetic.  At the
time of writing, the argument has not received external peer review.  It
should therefore be treated as a reproducible computer-assisted proof draft
pending external verification.
